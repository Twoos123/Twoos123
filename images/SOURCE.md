# Mirage images

These are Counter-Strike 2 images, the property of **Valve Corporation**, used here for a
non-commercial, community profile. Counter-Strike® is a registered trademark of Valve Corporation.

- `mirage.jpg`: one of Valve's CS2 screenshots from the game's Steam store page, the view from
  mid window on Mirage. Cropped to the banner's shape (1200px wide from 0,266 of the 1920px
  original). The two players standing in the shot were painted out.
- `mirage-t.png`: the player who stood in the archway in that same screenshot, cut out so the
  HUD can animate him.
- `mirage-radar.jpg`: CS2's Mirage radar overview (`de_mirage_radar`, from the game files). The
  playable 880px square is cropped from 100,80 of the 1024px original, brightened a little and
  flattened onto the radar's dark panel.

`scripts/hud.mjs` embeds all three in `assets/hud.svg`.
